Sources & method

An archive,
with its gaps.

From The New York Public Library. This edition selects menus, postcards, pamphlets and cigarette cards from NYPL’s public-domain metadata release. It does not include every digitized item or a live search of today’s catalogue.

Dates belong to different things

The source describes the collection as it stood on 30 December 2015. The download date tells you when CoolCode retrieved that archive. A record’s date describes the original item and may be approximate or span several years. Date filters use overlapping recorded ranges; the chart uses the earliest year.

Places are recorded, not inferred

Place search includes geographic subjects and publication places. Many menus have no structured place metadata. The main search also looks inside titles, notes and subjects. No coordinates or locations are invented.

Rights and original scans

NYPL identified these items as having no known U.S. copyright restrictions in this release; its metadata is CC0. Each record links to the original for current details. Scan previews are optional, requested from NYPL, and may be unavailable. The decorative paper at the top is an illustration, not a scanned historical object.

Local search, public evidence

Your searches and comparison choices stay in this tab. Loading previews or opening source links contacts NYPL. Saving a record downloads its metadata, not the original image. No account or key is required.

The original API has retired. This tool uses NYPL’s downloadable archive, so its search remains usable without that service. Refreshes are manual because the source is a fixed historical release.
